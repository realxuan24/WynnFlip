How to interpret these signals

Repeated observations show that the same listing ID appeared across scans; this can indicate lingering inventory, but not that nobody wants it.

Price reductions mean the observed ask fell for the same ID. They suggest seller repricing, not a confirmed failed sale.

New supply counts first-time observed IDs, not every item newly posted in-game.
